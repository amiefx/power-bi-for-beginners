# Lesson 1.2 — Desktop, Service, Mobile

▶️ **Video:** <video link>

Sara, the Sales Manager at Contoso, asks: "Which Power BI do I need? And where will I see the report?" In this lesson you meet the three parts of Power BI and learn what each one is for: build, share and view.

## What you'll learn
- **Power BI Desktop:** the free app on your PC where you build reports
- **Power BI Service:** the website where you share reports with other people
- **Power BI Mobile:** the phone app for viewing reports
- Which part you need, and which part Sara needs
- What's free: Desktop is free, and almost all of this course is free. To share in the service, you need a paid license.

## Files
This lesson has no start or end file. You only look at a blank report in Power BI Desktop, and nothing is changed or saved. You install Power BI Desktop in the next lesson (1.3); the setup is also in the [main README](../../../README.md).

## The lesson in short
1. Power BI isn't one app. It has three parts that work together. Follow one report: you **build** it on your PC, you **share** it on a website, and Sara **views** it in her browser or on her phone.
2. **Power BI Desktop** is the free app on your PC where you build reports. It runs on Windows. In Desktop you connect to data, clean it and build the report pages. You'll work in Desktop for most of this course. Your report is a file on your PC, but only you can see it there.
3. A first look at Desktop, with a blank report: on the **Home** tab, **Get data** connects you to your data. On the white page you build your visuals. When your report is ready, **Publish** sends it to a website. You'll use these buttons later.
4. **Power BI Service** is the website where you share reports with other people. You open it in a browser and sign in with a work or school account. Sara and her team open your report there. They don't need to install anything.
5. Good news: Desktop is free, and you can follow almost all of this course for free. But to share in the service, you need a paid license.
6. **Power BI Mobile** is the phone app for viewing reports. It's for iPhone and iPad, and for Android phones and tablets. You view reports there; you don't build them.
7. On a computer, a Windows PC or a Mac, you view reports in the service, in your browser.
8. Sara's answer: you build the report in Desktop and share it in the service. Sara doesn't need Desktop at all. She views the report in her browser, or in the Mobile app on her phone.

⚠️ **Common mistake:** looking for a Power BI app for Windows to view reports. That old app, only for viewing, is no longer available. On a Windows PC or a Mac, open reports in the service, in your browser. (This is not Power BI Desktop: Desktop is where you build.)

## Your turn
Which part of Power BI do you use:
1. to build a visual?
2. to open a report on a Mac?
3. to check a report on your phone?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

1. **Build a visual:** Power BI Desktop, the free app on your Windows PC.
2. **Open a report on a Mac:** the Power BI Service, in a browser. On a Windows PC or a Mac, you view reports in the service. (The Power BI Windows app, an old app only for viewing, is no longer available. Desktop runs on Windows and is where you build.)
3. **Check a report on your phone:** Power BI Mobile, the phone app for viewing reports (iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets).

Just like Sara: she doesn't need Desktop. She views reports in the service or in the Mobile app.
</details>
