This is the start of a free Power BI course for beginners. You see the report you'll build at the end, meet the story behind the course, and get the plan for all 11 chapters.

## What you'll learn
- What you'll build: the final report that answers Sara's big question, "Why did our sales fall in 2024?"

- **Power BI** is a Microsoft tool that turns data into reports.
- The roadmap: 11 chapters after this welcome, from installing Power BI to the final project
- How the course works: 67 short lessons (most are 5-8 minutes), free course files, a **Your turn** challenge and subtitles in every lesson

## Your turn
Contoso sold **$43.8 million** in 2023 and only **$31.6 million** in 2024. Why do you think sales fell? Fewer stores? Lower prices? Write your guess down. You'll check it in the final project.

<details>
<summary>Answer (spoiler for the final project)</summary>

There is no wrong guess: the final project checks it with the report. Here is what the data shows (2024 compared with 2023):
- **Not fewer stores:** no store closed in 2023 or 2024, so the same stores sold less.
- **Not lower prices:** the margin stayed at about 56% (55.9%), so prices did not fall compared with costs. The problem is fewer sales.
- **The physical stores fell most:** from $20.2M to $12.1M (-40%). Online fell less: from $23.6M to $19.6M (-17%), so online grew from 54% to 62% of sales.
- The **United States** stores lost the most dollars (-$4.8M), and **Computers** was the biggest category drop (-35%).
</details>
